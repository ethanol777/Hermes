## Useful answer framing from this session
- For MoE-vs-dense comparisons, anchor on capacity/compute decoupling:
  - dense FFN: capacity and compute both grow
  - MoE Top-1: total capacity grows while per-token compute stays near the base model
- For N=8 vs N=4 under equal training steps, a safe defense is:
  - current result only proves N=4 is more robust under the given budget
  - larger expert counts may need more steps, more data, or different balancing settings
  - do not claim “more experts is always better”
- For expert specialization questions, avoid pretending fine-grained task-expert visualization exists if it was not done.
  - Say the current evidence is routing statistics, usage ratios, entropy, and ablations.
  - Frame task-level expert maps as future work.
